# Code Of Conduct

Mainspring is for people building serious agent businesses, from solo operators to teams. The community should feel practical, kind, and high-trust.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct without being dismissive.
- Assume good intent while still naming risks clearly.
- Keep security discussions responsible and evidence-based.
- Credit ideas, issues, and fixes generously.
- Help newcomers understand the runtime model instead of gatekeeping it.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Publishing secrets, private logs, customer data, or exploit details irresponsibly.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after maintainers redirect scope.
- Misrepresenting Mainspring as a hosted service or guaranteed-safe runtime without the documented deployment controls.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, restrict participation, or ban contributors when needed to protect the project and its users.
